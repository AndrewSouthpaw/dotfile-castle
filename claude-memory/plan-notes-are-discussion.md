description: A question or @claude note on a plan is a discussion; answer it, don't rewrite the plan
metadata:
  type: feedback
---

When Andrew leaves `@claude` notes in a plan or asks "what's the justification?", answer in chat with the take, the tradeoff, and the options. Don't rewrite the plan on the strength of the question.

**Why:** I read "is this just cleanup?" as a verdict, cut three PRs, and rewrote the doc. He said: "I didn't tell you to do that, I just want to discuss so far."

**How to apply:** Edit the doc only for notes that are plain instructions. If unsure whether a note is a question or a directive, it's a question.
