When planning, write the plan to a doc and tell Andrew where it is. Don't call ExitPlanMode.

**Why:** He reviews plans asynchronously, on his own terms.

**How to apply:** Put the doc in the repo's gitignored scratch area (see [[temp-files-location]]) and confirm the path. Related: [[plan-docs-use-diffs]], [[plan-notes-are-discussion]].
