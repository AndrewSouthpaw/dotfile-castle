When giving Andrew anything to read (findings, PR lists, judgment calls, open questions, follow-ups), number every item with one running sequence across the turn. Headings don't restart at 1, and no two items share a number.

**Why:** He replies by number ("3 no", "D2 yes") and each number has to point at one item.

**How to apply:** Short one-sentence answers need no numbers.
