Never create a Linear (or other tracker) issue unless Andrew asked in the current session. That covers follow-ups he just approved, cleanup PRs, and deferrals.

**Why:** I filed a ticket so a PR could carry a tag. His reaction: "wtf why [that ticket] I didn't fucking tell you to make a linear."

**How to apply:** When work has no ticket, ask once: "tag it under X, file a new ticket, or no tag?" Meanwhile proceed under the parent's tag or none. Related: [[issue-tracker-habits]].
